|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-03-01 | Tampering | Thai-ID checksum (missing final mod 10) | mitigate | `(11-(sum%11))%10`; check-digit-0 valid vectors + I6 trap fail any implementation that drops the final mod 10. |
| T-03-02 | Tampering | identifier silently corrupted/validated by an LLM | mitigate | Validators are pure with no LLM import path — structurally impossible (Pitfall 5 / §A3). |
| T-03-03 | Tampering | over/under-strict validators (reject valid foreign/Thai-script) | accept→mitigate | Charset/length ranges are pragmatic A4 placeholders; vectors prove Thai-script plate, Lao plate, and foreign passport are accepted; ranges flagged for later confirmation. |
